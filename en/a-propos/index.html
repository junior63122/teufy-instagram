About us

Teufy, the nightlife guide to the French Riviera

A guide checked every day, an Instagram media and a network of 200+ partners, from Nice to Monaco.

Our mission

On the French Riviera, parties are everywhere: Shotgun, DICE, Resident Advisor, Weezevent, club websites, city calendars, promoters’ stories… but never in one place. Teufy brings them all together, with no duplicates, and checks each one on its official page (date, time, venue, price) before publishing it.

Our goal: in two seconds, you know where to go out tonight, whether you are in Nice, Cannes, Monaco, Antibes or anywhere else on the coast.

Director

Junior Joyant

Born in Nice and a true party lover, Junior Joyant created Teufy for a simple reason: on the French Riviera, parties were everywhere except in one place. Today he leads a team of about ten people who sort, check and tell the story of the night from Nice to Monaco.
