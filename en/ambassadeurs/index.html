Ambassadors

Become a Teufy ambassador

Always know about the best parties before anyone else? Are you the one your crew asks "where are we going tonight?" Represent Teufy in your city or at your uni.

The role

Scout

Tip us off to parties, new spots, and event drops in your city or school before anyone else hears about them.

Share

Drop Teufy's picks into your group chats, uni cohorts, and student clubs.

Create

If you're into it: shoot photos, videos, and stories on nights out for the Teufy account (with the promoters' green light, of course).

Connect

Hook us up with student boards, promoters, and artists in your network.

What's in it for you

Be part of the team

Join the Teufy squad and see how an events media platform works from the inside.

Real experience for your CV

Scouting, social media, relations with promoters: awesome stuff to boost your resume, portfolio, or internship search.

The network

Get to know promoters, clubs, DJs, and Teufy's 200+ partners.

Nightlife perks

Whenever our partners offer them: guestlist spots, free passes, and discounts exclusively for ambassadors.

Who we're looking for

  • Be 18 or older
  • Live or study on the French Riviera (Nice, Cannes, Antibes, Monaco, Menton, Sophia…)
  • Go out regularly and love sharing the best plans
  • Be active on Instagram
  • Be reliable: we only share verified info

How to apply

Slide into our Instagram DMs with: your first name, age, city, school or job, your Insta handle, and two sentences on why you want to repp Teufy.
