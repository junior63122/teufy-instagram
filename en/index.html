Frequently asked questions

How does Teufy find the parties?

Every day, Teufy goes through ticket platforms (Shotgun, DICE, Resident Advisor, Weezevent…), club websites, city calendars and promoters’ accounts, removes duplicates and checks each party on its official page.

Can I buy tickets on Teufy?

No: the “Tickets” button takes you straight to the promoter’s official ticket page, with no commission. When a party is sold on several sites, Teufy compares the prices.

How do I find a shatta, techno or afro party?

Type the style in the search bar or use the filters. Each style also has its own page: techno, house, afro, shatta, latino, rap.

How do I add my party?

Send the poster, date and ticket link to @teufy_official on Instagram: we check it and add it for free.
