Student Unions · Associations

Your student nights, taken to the next level

Student unions, sports clubs, arts societies, student associations from Université Côte d'Azur, Sophia Antipolis, and schools across Nice, Cannes, and Monaco: Teufy helps you pack your parties and get them organized.

What Teufy offers you

Your parties on the calendar, for free

Your party shows up on the website, on the map, on your city's page, and on Google, with a direct link to your ticketing (HelloAsso, Shotgun, Weezevent…).

A spotlight on Instagram

Standout student parties get featured in Teufy's picks, in collab with your account.

The right venue for your party

Teufy works with over 200 clubs, bars, beach clubs, and venues across the French Riviera: we connect you with the ones that host student nights.

Negotiated deals

Whenever possible, we help you get better deals from our partners for your events (private bookings, group rates).

An ambassador at your school

A board member can become a Teufy ambassador and get priority coverage for your events.

How it works

  1. 1

    Drop us a line

    Association name, school, Instagram handle, and your upcoming dates.

  2. 2

    We build it together

    Calendar listing, promo, venue hunting: we see what you need.

  3. 3

    Your parties are live

    Send us every new date: we'll add it and spread the word.
