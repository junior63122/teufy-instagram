Frequently asked questions

Does it cost anything?

Listing your event on the guide is completely free. For dedicated content (reels, sponsored posts, campaigns), we'll put together a custom package for you.

How do I add my event?

Send the flyer, date, time, venue, and ticket link via DM to @teufy_official. We'll check it out and add it.

My party is already on Shotgun or DICE, is it still useful?

Yes: Teufy puts it in front of people who aren't following you yet—on Instagram, on Google, and in six languages—and links straight to your ticket shop.

Some info is wrong or my flyer has changed

Hit us up: we'll fix it the same day.
